Pre-turn same-sscat clicks (mean) by order outcome
Depth predicts ordering: sessions that end in an order carried ~1.5 more same-sscat clicks before the turn (FIND 6.22 vs 4.66; DECIDE 5.41 vs 3.81). Deepest of all: buyers of the same sscat but a different product (FIND 7.78, median 4) — serial comparators for whom the Vaani PDP was one more candidate. And given an ordered session, the order is the turn's own product in 42.8% of DECIDE turns vs only 16.4% of FIND turns.

Distribution view: non-ordering sessions are front-loaded (FIND 36.7% / DECIDE 43.1% of turns on the 1st PDP), while ordering sessions shift right — same-sscat-different-product buyers most of all (only 21.3% of FIND turns on the 1st PDP, and 22.2% on the 11th+). The same-product buyers sit in between: engaged early-ish, but with a fatter deep tail than non-buyers.

Turn on the very first same-sscat PDP: 8.5% (FIND) / 9.8% (DECIDE) of sessions go on to order — 91%+ order nothing. FIND first-PDP orders are mostly elsewhere (4.8% diff sscat + 2.6% same sscat diff product vs just 1.1% the turn's own product), while DECIDE first-PDP orders are led by the turn's own product (4.0%). Conversion rises steadily with depth — FIND 8.5% → 15.2%, DECIDE 9.8% → 24.3% at 11+ PDPs — and for DECIDE the same-product share more than doubles (4.0% → 9.7%): deep-crawl decide-talk is the highest-intent signal in this data. (Correlation, not causation — depth proxies intent.)

Same-product purchases happen on the spot: median post-turn same-sscat clicks = 0 (DECIDE 72.8% buy with zero further same-sscat clicks; mean just 0.89). Different-product purchases mean the turn was mid-crawl — 6.3 (DECIDE) to 8.4 (FIND) more same-sscat PDPs follow, and the distribution shows it: only 1.5–3.2% of different-product buyers order with zero further clicks, while ~25% (FIND) click through 11+ more PDPs. Revisiting the Vaani PDP is rare (~80% never re-open it); only FIND-same-product buyers meaningfully return (41.8% ≥1 revisit — talk, wander, come back to buy).
