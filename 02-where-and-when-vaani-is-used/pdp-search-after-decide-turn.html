Why does a search get triggered on a DECIDE turn?

Vaani · 2026-08-10 → 2026-08-18 · 14,266 turns where the buyer deliberated on a product page and a search was then rendered — by Vaani on the same turn, or by the buyer asking on the next one

How category movement is measured. Not by mapping the search query to an sscat — that fails audit here, because the taxonomy is granular enough that Sarees → “cotton saree” resolves to a different sscat, and the matcher also produces plain errors (“denim co ord set” → Diwan Cover Sets). Instead: the product in focus on the following turn where that is observable, falling back to whether the query shares any word with the anchor's own category name. The exact test is never overridden by the fallback.

Scope. Of these 6,465 turns, 2,638 (40.8%) were still on the anchor's own product page when the buyer asked, and 3,827 had moved onto the feed. Both are kept — the behaviour is the same, only the screen changed underneath them. Turn N+1 follow-ups that were themselves DECIDE questions are excluded: another question about the same product is not a search ask. The search must have actually rendered, which is what makes Vaani's own behaviour corroborate the FIND label.
Population: DECIDE turns on a product page (SINGLE_PRODUCT with a product in focus) that led to a rendered search. Prices are catalogue prices from the 2026-08-18 snapshot, not amounts paid. Decide topic comes from a rule classifier (87.7% precision on the DECIDE branch, 95.5% on the topic); the taxonomy's L3 layer is not used anywhere on this page. No experiment filter — Control and Test are pooled. Every session here placed at least one order. Row data: data-outputs/search_cause_rows_20260820.csv.
