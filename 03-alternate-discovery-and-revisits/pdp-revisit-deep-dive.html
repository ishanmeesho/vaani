Merged: revisit × re-discovery quantum + conversion

Re-discovery — for the PIDs clicked in this final session, did the user click at least one of those PIDs in any of their last 3 click-having prior sessions? Prior sessions here means sessions with real clicks — no-click / garbage sessions are skipped.
  • rediscovery_yes — user had 3 prior click sessions AND overlap ≥ 1 PID with this session.
  • rediscovery_no — user had 3 prior click sessions AND overlap = 0 PIDs.
  • no_3_priors — user had fewer than 3 prior click sessions in the 2026-07-02 → 2026-07-28 window; re-discovery is not computable for them.
Quantum = share of the same 1,013,017 first-click sessions in each (revisit × re-discovery) combo. Conversion = share of that combo that placed an order.
